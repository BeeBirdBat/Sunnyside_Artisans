There are specific books related to our activities that may not be readily available in libraries, or we may prefer to have easy access for reference. Therefore, creating our own library would be beneficial. Additionally, the following books offer a glimpse into the types of activities we will be involved in at Sunnyside Artisans. Please contact Sunnyside Artisans if you are interested in establishing this library and accessing these books. Perhaps you have one of these books or a similar book you would like to donate. I will also donate some books to the cause. We can expand the list of books we aim to acquire.
Many of these authors have been featured on podcasts and given presentations that can be found online if you are interested in learning more about them.
